# Academic writing and proposals: how far to go

In formal registers, the general advice ("use contractions", "cut hedges", "short punchy sentences") goes too far. Here, sounding human means sounding like a competent researcher or a credible organiser: precise, direct, grounded in the author's own sources and numbers.

## 1. Never change

- Citations (author, year, page, order, citation style), reference lists
- Numbers, units, percentages, statistics, p-values, confidence intervals, sample sizes, currency amounts (RAB)
- Direct quotes, equations, variable names, model names, code
- Table/figure/appendix references ("Tabel 2", "Figure 3")
- Field terminology and defined terms, even if they appear in a "tell" list (*sustainable development*, *ekonomi sirkular*, *robust standard errors*, *signifikan pada α = 5%*)
- The argument itself: don't add claims, drop caveats the author needs, or strengthen a conclusion beyond the data

Never add a citation or a source, even a plausible one. If a sentence makes a claim that clearly needs a source and has none, leave it and mention it in the one trailing line.

## 2. Academic English: tells and fixes

| Tell | Why it reads as AI | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "In recent years, X has garnered significant attention" | announces the field instead of the gap | state the finding or gap with its citation |
| "This study aims to shed light on / delve into" | stock verbs | "This study estimates / tests / compares..." |
| "plays a pivotal / crucial role in" | vague significance | say the effect: "accounts for 40% of...", "determines whether..." |
| "a growing body of literature" without the literature | weasel attribution | cite the studies, or "Several studies (A, 2020; B, 2022)..." |
| "underscores the importance of", "highlights the need for" closers | moral-of-the-paragraph | end on the evidence, or state the specific implication |
| "offers valuable insights", "provides a comprehensive overview" | self-praise | say what the reader learns |
| "the intricate interplay between", "multifaceted", "nuanced understanding" | filler adjectives | name the relationship |
| "Furthermore, ... Moreover, ... Additionally, ..." | connector chain | one real connector, or restructure |
| "significant / significantly" for big or important | ambiguous in research | reserve for statistical significance |
| "paving the way for", "bridging the gap" | clichés | "makes X possible", "addresses the lack of..." |
| "It is imperative that", "It is important to note that" | throat-clearing | state the point |
| "Future research should explore..." generic list | template ending | name one concrete next study tied to a limitation |
| ", thereby ensuring / highlighting ..." tails | -ing/-by tails | split or cut |

Keep: passive voice in methods where the field expects it, "we" if the author uses it, precise hedges, the paper's own structure.

## 3. Academic Indonesian: tells and fixes

| Tell | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Dewasa ini, isu X semakin menjadi perhatian berbagai pihak." | open with the problem and its source: "Emisi sektor energi naik [X]% dalam lima tahun terakhir ([sumber])." |
| "Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis secara komprehensif..." | "Penelitian ini menganalisis..." (keep *bertujuan* once if the template needs it; drop *secara komprehensif*) |
| "memegang peranan yang sangat penting dan krusial" | "berperan" + the actual effect |
| "Berbagai penelitian telah menunjukkan bahwa..." with no citation | cite what the author has, or narrow the claim |
| "Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa..." after every finding | vary: "Artinya...", "Temuan ini sejalan dengan...", or let the next sentence interpret |
| "Selain itu,... Lebih lanjut,... Di sisi lain,..." | keep the logical one, delete or restructure the rest |
| "..., di mana ..." / "yang mana" | "yang", "tempat", or split the sentence |
| "melakukan analisis terhadap", "melakukan pengujian" | "menganalisis", "menguji" |
| "terdiri dari", "berdasarkan pada", "adalah merupakan" | "terdiri atas", "berdasarkan", "adalah/merupakan" |
| "signifikan" for big | reserve for statistical significance; else give the number or "cukup besar" |
| "Dengan demikian, dapat disimpulkan bahwa..." ending every paragraph | only in the actual conclusion |
| addressing "kita" / "Anda" | keep the author's *peneliti* / *penulis* / passive |

Baku matters in skripsi/tesis/jurnal: follow KBBI and EYD Edisi V. Don't over-formalise either: *adalah*, *yaitu*, *karena* are fine; you don't need *dikarenakan* or *oleh sebab itu maka*.

## 4. Hedging: calibrate, don't delete

Academic hedges carry information. The AI tell is stacking and blanket hedging, not hedging itself.

- Stacked: "may potentially suggest" → "suggests". "mungkin dapat berpotensi menyebabkan" → "dapat menyebabkan".
- Blanket: a hedge on every sentence, including established facts → remove from the facts, keep on the inference.
- Under-claimed: "it could be argued that the results might indicate" on a clear, well-powered result → "the results indicate".
- Over-claimed (also fix it): correlational data + "proves" / "membuktikan" → "is associated with" / "berhubungan dengan". Cross-sectional data + "causes" → "is linked to".

## 5. Section-by-section notes

- **Abstract:** one sentence each for gap, aim, method, main result (with the number), implication. Cut "This paper delves into...", "valuable insights", "comprehensive". No citations unless the journal allows.
- **Introduction / Pendahuluan / Latar belakang:** start with the problem and its evidence, then the gap, then the aim. No "era" openers.
- **Literature review / Tinjauan pustaka:** organise by argument, not by paper ("Studi A menemukan... Studi B menemukan..." for ten paragraphs is a list, not a review). Let sentences compare: "Berbeda dengan A (2019), B (2022) menemukan...". Don't add papers.
- **Methods / Metode:** plain, chronological, replicable. Passive is fine. Almost no hedging needed. Cut adjectives ("rigorous", "comprehensive", "cermat").
- **Results / Hasil:** numbers first, interpretation light. No "interestingly", "notably", "menariknya".
- **Discussion / Pembahasan:** this is where hedges belong. Compare with the cited literature, explain mechanisms, admit limits.
- **Conclusion / Kesimpulan:** answer the research questions directly, one concrete implication, one concrete next step. No "future is bright", no new claims.

## 6. Proposals and reports

Readers are funders, judges, lecturers or partner institutions. They skim and want: what, who, how many, when, where, how much, and why this team.

- **Latar belakang:** problem + evidence + why this activity addresses it. No era openers, no "tidak dapat dipungkiri".
- **Tujuan:** verbs that can be checked ("menyeleksi 30 tim", "menghasilkan 10 purwarupa"), not "meningkatkan semangat dan kreativitas".
- **Sasaran / Target peserta:** numbers and criteria.
- **Manfaat:** different benefits for different parties, not "Kegiatan ini diharapkan dapat..." five times.
- **Waktu, tempat, susunan acara, RAB:** never change the figures. Fix only the prose around them.
- **Penutup:** one or two sentences: the ask (support, sponsorship, approval) and a contact. Not "Demikian proposal ini kami buat, semoga dapat bermanfaat bagi kita semua" piled on top of three more pleasantries. A single standard closing line ("Demikian proposal ini kami sampaikan. Atas perhatian dan dukungan Bapak/Ibu, kami ucapkan terima kasih.") is convention in Indonesian proposals and can stay.

Formal Indonesian proposal conventions that are NOT AI tells and should stay when present: *Bapak/Ibu*, *Dengan hormat*, the standard closing above, the institution's required section names, *Hormat kami*.

## 7. Before/after examples

**Academic EN, introduction**

Before:
> In recent years, the circular economy has garnered significant attention as a pivotal strategy for fostering sustainable development. It is not merely an environmental initiative, but a comprehensive paradigm that encompasses economic, social, and environmental dimensions. Furthermore, numerous studies have highlighted its transformative potential (Geissdoerfer et al., 2017; Kirchherr et al., 2017). This study aims to delve into the intricate interplay between circular economy practices and firm performance in Indonesian SMEs.

After:
> Circular economy practices are often promoted as a way to cut waste without cutting growth (Geissdoerfer et al., 2017; Kirchherr et al., 2017). Most of the evidence, however, comes from large firms in Europe. Whether the same practices pay off for small manufacturers in Indonesia is largely untested. This study examines the relationship between circular economy practices and firm performance in Indonesian SMEs.

(Note: the claim "most of the evidence comes from large firms in Europe" must come from the author's own lit review. If it doesn't, keep a neutral version, e.g. "Evidence on SMEs remains limited", and say so in the trailing line.)

**Academic EN, results/discussion**

Before:
> The results indicate that green credit significantly impacts renewable energy investment, underscoring the crucial role of financial institutions in driving the energy transition. This highlights the importance of robust policy frameworks.

After (when the coefficient is statistically significant):
> Green credit has a positive and statistically significant effect on renewable energy investment (β = [ ], p < [ ]). Lending decisions by banks therefore affect how fast renewable capacity gets built, so rules that change how banks lend are likely to change investment as well.

(Fill the coefficient and p-value from the author's own table if the paragraph should carry them; never make them up. The second sentence only restates "importance of robust policy frameworks" concretely. It adds no new claim.)

**Academic ID, latar belakang**

Before:
> Di era globalisasi yang semakin pesat ini, isu perubahan iklim menjadi perhatian berbagai pihak. Tidak dapat dipungkiri bahwa ekonomi hijau memegang peranan yang sangat penting dalam mewujudkan pembangunan berkelanjutan. Selain itu, ekonomi hijau tidak hanya berdampak pada lingkungan, tetapi juga pada aspek sosial dan ekonomi. Oleh karena itu, penelitian ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis secara komprehensif faktor-faktor yang mempengaruhi penerapan ekonomi hijau di Indonesia.

After:
> Indonesia menargetkan penurunan emisi sebesar [X]% pada 2030 ([sumber]), tetapi pembiayaan proyek rendah karbon masih jauh dari kebutuhan. Ekonomi hijau sering diajukan sebagai jalan keluar karena menggabungkan target lingkungan dengan pertumbuhan dan lapangan kerja. Masalahnya, penerapannya di daerah sangat tidak merata. Penelitian ini menganalisis faktor-faktor yang memengaruhi penerapan ekonomi hijau di Indonesia.

(The original says almost nothing concrete, so a real rewrite needs content. The brackets and the claims about financing and uneven uptake show the *shape* to aim for. In a real rewrite, build those sentences only from what the author's sources or other sections actually say. Fill the brackets from those sources, or leave them and flag.)

**Academic ID, tinjauan pustaka**

Before:
> Penelitian yang dilakukan oleh Sari (2020) menunjukkan bahwa green finance memiliki pengaruh yang signifikan terhadap kinerja perusahaan. Selain itu, penelitian yang dilakukan oleh Pratama (2021) juga menunjukkan hasil yang sama. Lebih lanjut, penelitian yang dilakukan oleh Wijaya (2022) menunjukkan bahwa green finance tidak berpengaruh terhadap kinerja perusahaan. Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa masih terdapat perbedaan hasil penelitian.

After:
> Temuan tentang *green finance* dan kinerja perusahaan belum seragam. Sari (2020) dan Pratama (2021) menemukan pengaruh positif yang signifikan, sedangkan Wijaya (2022) tidak menemukan pengaruh. Perbedaan ini mungkin berkaitan dengan sampel: dua studi pertama meneliti perbankan, sementara Wijaya (2022) meneliti perusahaan manufaktur.

(The last sentence is an interpretation. Only include something like it if the author's text or notes support it; otherwise stop after the second sentence.)

**Proposal ID, latar belakang + tujuan**

Before:
> Di era Revolusi Industri 4.0, mahasiswa dituntut untuk menjadi generasi yang unggul, kreatif, inovatif, dan berdaya saing global. Oleh karena itu, kami menyelenggarakan kegiatan ini sebagai wadah bagi mahasiswa untuk mengembangkan potensi diri. Kegiatan ini diharapkan dapat memberikan manfaat yang signifikan bagi semua pihak.
>
> Tujuan:
> 1. Meningkatkan kreativitas dan inovasi mahasiswa.
> 2. Mempererat sinergi dan kolaborasi antarmahasiswa ASEAN.
> 3. Menciptakan generasi muda yang berdaya saing global.

After:
> Mahasiswa teknik di kawasan ASEAN jarang punya kesempatan menguji purwarupa mereka di depan industri. Kompetisi kampus umumnya berhenti di tahap proposal, sehingga ide yang menjanjikan tidak pernah dibangun. Kegiatan ini mempertemukan tim mahasiswa dari [jumlah] negara ASEAN untuk membangun dan mempresentasikan purwarupa kepada juri dari [mitra industri].
>
> Tujuan:
> 1. Menyeleksi [jumlah] tim dari [jumlah] negara melalui tahap proposal.
> 2. Mendampingi tim finalis membangun purwarupa selama [durasi].
> 3. Mempertemukan finalis dengan [jumlah] mitra industri untuk peluang pendanaan atau magang.

(Trailing line to the user: "Isi angka dan nama mitra di dalam tanda kurung.")

**Proposal ID, penutup**

Before:
> Demikian proposal ini kami buat dengan sebenar-benarnya. Kami berharap kegiatan ini dapat terlaksana dengan baik dan memberikan manfaat yang sebesar-besarnya bagi semua pihak. Besar harapan kami agar Bapak/Ibu dapat berpartisipasi dalam menyukseskan kegiatan ini. Atas perhatian dan kerja samanya, kami ucapkan terima kasih.

After:
> Kami mengajukan dukungan sponsor sebesar Rp[jumlah] sesuai rincian pada RAB (Lampiran 2). Untuk diskusi lebih lanjut, Bapak/Ibu dapat menghubungi [nama] di [nomor]. Atas perhatian dan dukungan Bapak/Ibu, kami ucapkan terima kasih.
