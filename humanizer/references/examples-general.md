# Before / after examples

Grouped by where the text is going. Notice that most "after" versions are shorter, more specific, and less tidy.

## WhatsApp / text to a friend or colleague

**Before:** "Hey! I just wanted to let you know that I'm running a little bit late today due to unexpected traffic. I should be there in approximately 15 minutes. Apologies for any inconvenience!"
**After:** "Stuck in traffic on the causeway, 15 min late sorry"

**Before:** "That sounds like an amazing opportunity! I'm truly so happy for you. You absolutely deserve it, and I'm confident you'll do incredibly well in this new role."
**After:** "Eh congrats!! You've been chasing this for ages. Drinks this weekend?"

## Work email

**Before:**
> Subject: Following Up on Our Previous Discussion
> I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to follow up on our discussion last week regarding the Q3 marketing budget. As we discussed, it's crucial that we align on the key priorities moving forward. Please let me know your thoughts at your earliest convenience.

**After:**
> Subject: Q3 marketing budget
> Hi Daniel, picking up from last Tuesday. I still need your call on whether we keep the trade-show spend or move it to ads. Could you let me know by Friday so I can lock the numbers?

**Before (declining):** "Thank you so much for thinking of me for this exciting opportunity. After careful consideration, I've decided that I won't be able to take this on at this time. I truly appreciate your understanding."
**After:** "Thanks for asking me. I'm going to pass on this one, I've got too much on until December. If it comes round again next year I'd be keen."

## LinkedIn / social post

**Before:**
> I'm thrilled to announce that I've embarked on a new journey as Senior Analyst at Acme! 🚀 This role isn't just a job, it's an opportunity to leverage my passion for data and drive meaningful impact. Grateful for everyone who supported me along the way. The best is yet to come! 💡

**After:**
> Started at Acme this week as a senior analyst. First task is untangling three years of sales data that lives in eleven different spreadsheets, which is exactly the kind of mess I like. Thanks to Priya and Hafiz for the referral.

## Essay / report paragraph

**Before:**
> Renewable energy plays a pivotal role in today's ever-evolving energy landscape. It's not just about reducing emissions; it's about fostering sustainable economic growth, enhancing energy security, and empowering communities. Moreover, the transition presents numerous opportunities for innovation. Ultimately, embracing renewables is crucial for a brighter future.

**After:**
> Malaysia's rooftop solar grew [X-fold] between [year] and [year], mostly through net-metering schemes. The emissions case is well known. The less discussed point is cost: for a factory running daytime shifts, rooftop panels pay for themselves in [N] years, which is why manufacturers took them up faster than the targets assumed.

(Note the brackets: the rewrite wants specifics, but the original had none. Pull the real numbers from the user's sources, or leave the brackets and ask. Never invent figures to sound concrete.)

## Product / bio copy

**Before:** "Our cutting-edge platform seamlessly integrates with your existing workflow, empowering teams to unlock their full potential and take productivity to the next level."
**After:** "It plugs into Slack and Google Drive, so your team stops copying things between tabs."

**Before (bio):** "A passionate and dedicated professional with a proven track record of driving results in dynamic, fast-paced environments."
**After:** "I run ops for a 40-person logistics company in JB. Before that, six years in procurement. I like fixing processes nobody else wants to touch."

## Answer to a question

**Before:** "Great question! When it comes to learning to code, there are several key factors to consider. First, it's important to choose the right language. Second, consistent practice is crucial. Finally, building real projects is essential. Ultimately, the best approach depends on your unique goals!"
**After:** "Pick one language (Python is the easy default) and build something you actually want, like a script that renames your photos. Tutorials alone won't stick. You learn when you get stuck on your own project."
